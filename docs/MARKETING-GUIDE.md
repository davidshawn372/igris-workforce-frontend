# IGRIS Workforce — Marketing Guide

## Positioning
An interactive onboarding workspace that helps companies turn scattered knowledge into a clear first-week learning path. Two connected experiences: people teams build and manage the journey; new hires learn, practice and track their next step.

## Who to reach
Start with small growing companies, founders handling onboarding themselves, and people-operations managers welcoming recurring cohorts. Interview a narrow group first, such as remote software teams. The product hypothesis is clearer onboarding and fewer repeated questions; demand and willingness to pay still need validation.

## What to show
- A manager builds or edits a role-specific journey.
- A new hire opens that journey and completes a checklist or quiz.
- The manager sees the changed completion progress.
- Knowledge resources are connected to the learning experience.
- Pro reporting compares teams and exports completion data.

## Price story
Core onboarding and employee learning stay free. Proposed Pro pricing is $7.99 USD per workspace per month for department comparisons, journey-stage analytics and completion reports. This is a simple starting offer to test, not evidence of profitability. Real billing has not been connected. Avoid selling unlimited AI usage at this price until service costs are understood.

## Honest promotion
Present the current build as an interactive frontend demo or early preview. Do not claim real AI automation, paying customers, guaranteed productivity gains, compliance certifications, live integrations or customer testimonials. Sample people and companies are fictional. The reference blossom media needs licensing/replacement before commercial campaigns.

## Launch approach
1. Make a 20–30 second product walkthrough using the actual interface, clearly labelled as a demo.
2. Show the problem in one sentence, then demonstrate a completed employee task and the manager's updated progress.
3. Post the walkthrough where founders and people-operations practitioners already discuss onboarding; tailor the message to the community.
4. Invite interested teams to a short feedback call or guided demo. Do not imply a signup form or public service exists before it does.
5. Ask what their current onboarding process costs in time, where new hires get stuck, and whether manager reporting is worth $7.99 monthly.
6. Track demo-to-conversation conversion, first journey created, first assignment and first completed lesson. Measure retention after the backend exists.

## Copy you can use
Headline: A better beginning for every new hire.
Description: Bring your company knowledge, practical tasks and learning progress together in one onboarding workspace.
Short post: A manual tells a new teammate what to know. IGRIS helps them see what to do next. Explore our interactive onboarding demo: build a journey, complete a lesson, and follow progress from both sides.
CTA now: Request a demo / Explore the frontend preview.
CTA after launch: Start your first onboarding journey.

## Questions to validate
Do managers need department comparisons yet, or would reusable journey templates be a stronger upgrade? Which tasks repeat for every hire? Who owns the budget? What integrations are essential? Use real interviews and product usage to adjust the offer rather than promising results before measuring them.
