# IGRIS Workforce — User Guide

## What it does
IGRIS gives new employees a clear place to start. Instead of searching through scattered manuals and messages, they follow a learning journey made of lessons, tasks, checklists and quizzes. Managers organize those journeys and see who may need help.

## For a company or manager
1. Open the company workspace from the landing page.
2. Browse Onboarding and choose a journey, or create a draft.
3. Edit its modules and publish it in the demo.
4. Open People to add a sample teammate and assign the appropriate journey.
5. Use the overview to follow progress and identify people asking for support.
6. Keep helpful material in Knowledge. The current upload demonstrates metadata only; it does not process your files.
7. Hiring provides a connected example of jobs and candidates before onboarding begins.

## For a new teammate
Open the employee portal, review your assigned path, and choose a lesson. Complete checklist items or answer a quiz, then mark eligible lessons complete. Progress appears in the manager workspace too. The assistant demonstrates finding answers in sample company documents; it is not a live AI service. Your profile and resource pages keep useful information close.

## Free and Pro
The free experience includes core onboarding, employee learning, company knowledge and basic progress. Pro is proposed at $7.99 USD per company workspace per month for manager analytics and downloadable learning progress reports. Employees do not pay separately.

The Plans screen currently offers a clearly marked Pro demo. It does not charge money, ask for payment details or create a subscription. End the demo on Plans, or refresh the page, to return to Free. Your onboarding data is kept.

## Finding your way
The labelled sidebar opens each company page. The header's Back to website link returns to the landing page, and the IGRIS logo remains clickable. Use the sun/moon button for the optional light theme; dark is the default. Search and filters help narrow lists.

## Demo limits
Changes stay in this browser. Other devices will not receive them. Invitations, AI, billing and external integrations are not live. Use fictional information while exploring. Workspace settings can reset the sample data, which removes your local demo changes.
